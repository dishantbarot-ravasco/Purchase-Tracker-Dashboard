Raw Material Store

Material comes into the store through MIR entry. Issue it to departments, take back what is not used, and record counts and write-offs here. Fields marked * are required.

Your account cannot issue material. Ask an admin for the Editor role at your plant.

1 Who it is for

The plant, the day it left the store, the department it went to and the person who took it.

2 What is going out

Find each material in this plant's stock and enter how much is going out, in its stock unit. Stock is taken from the oldest receipt first; the lots it comes from are shown under each line.

Your account cannot record returns. Ask an admin for the Editor role at your plant.

1 Which issue it comes back from

Material comes back against the issue it went out on, so it returns to the same receipts at the same value. Find the issue by its number, department, person or material.

2 What came back

For each material coming back, enter how much and why. Leave a line empty if nothing of it came back.

Your account cannot adjust stock. Ask an admin for the Editor role at your plant.

2 What changes

Pick what kind of change each line is, then the material. A physical count takes what you counted and adjusts by the difference from the books; add stock is for an opening balance or stock found; write off is for damage, loss or a sample.
