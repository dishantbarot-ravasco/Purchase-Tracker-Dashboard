Raw Material Store

Material comes into the store only through a MIR. To issue it, pick the MIR it came on and enter how much is going out - everything else comes from the MIR. Fields marked * are required.

Your account cannot issue material. Ask an admin for the Editor role at your plant.

1 Issue

The plant and the day the material left the store. Both are filled in for you; change them only if needed.

Optional details: department, person, production order, remarks

2 Pick the MIR

Find the MIR the material came in on - by MIR number, material, vendor, invoice or PO - or show every open MIR, and click a line (or "Add all lines"). Only MIRs with stock left at this plant are shown, oldest first. Lines from several MIRs can go on one issue.

3 How much goes out

Enter the quantity going out of each MIR, in its stock unit. It cannot be more than that MIR still holds on the issue date.

Your account cannot record returns.

Material back from

It goes back into the MIR it was issued from, at that MIR's rate. Leave a line empty if nothing of it came back.

Your account cannot record stock differences. Ask an admin for the Editor role at your plant.

Record a stock difference

For a MIR whose stock on the floor is not what the register says: a physical count (enter what is left of it - the difference is recorded) or a write-off (damage, loss, a sample). Material that went to production is an issue, not a difference.
