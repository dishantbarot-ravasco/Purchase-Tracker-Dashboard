4 What arrived

For each line, enter what came in and what the invoice charges. Quantities are in the PO line's own unit. If a figure differs from the PO, a box opens under the line - pick the closest reason and the MIR can still be saved.

    What this is for. When a MIR is saved with a difference from the PO - short, over or rejected quantity, a different rate or GST %, an invoice total that does not add up, or an unexpected tax type - the store picks a reason and the MIR is saved. Each difference then waits here for the purchase team to act on it (chase the balance, raise a debit note, amend the PO, return material) and resolve it with a note saying what was done. Nothing here blocks the receipt; it is the purchase team's follow-up list.
